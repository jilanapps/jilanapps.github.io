Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how Jilan Apps Official ("we", "us") handles information when you visit this website (jilanapps.github.io), including our App Store, Online Shopping Store and Business pages, and when you contact us. The site is run by Dr Asif, from Basti Malook, District Multan, Punjab, Pakistan.

Information we collect

We do not run user accounts on this website. We do not ask you to register, log in, or submit personal information through a form on this site. The information we receive is limited to the following:

  • Messages you send us. If you contact us by WhatsApp or email, we receive the details you share — usually your name, your phone number or email address, and what you are asking about. We use these details only to answer you, to process your order or request, and to fix any problem you report.
  • Order details. If you order from our Online Shopping Store, we receive the item code, your name, your delivery address and your phone number, so we can deliver your order. We do not receive or store any card or bank details, because payment is Cash on Delivery.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our pages may record basic technical information when you visit, such as the type of device and browser you use and the pages you visit. This is collected by the analytics and advertising services described below, and is used only to understand how the site is used and to improve it.

Google Analytics

Some pages on this site, including our store pages, use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site — for example, which pages are visited most and which apps are downloaded. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your device type, browser, approximate location (city or region level) and the pages you visit. It does not identify you by name. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by disabling cookies in your browser settings.

Advertising — Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads on this website. Google and its third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. For more information about how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Cookies

This website may use cookies for advertising (Google AdSense), for analytics (Google Analytics), and for basic site functions. Cookies are small files stored on your device. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some parts of the site may not work as well without them.

How we use your information

We use the information we receive only to: answer your messages; process and deliver your orders; build or fix an app or website you asked for; and understand how our site is used so we can improve it. We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone for marketing. We do not send you advertising messages unless you have asked us to.

How long we keep information

We keep order and message records only as long as needed to complete your order, answer your request, or fix a problem you reported. After that, we do not keep them longer than necessary. Analytics and advertising data is kept by Google under Google's own retention rules.

External links and third-party services

This website links to our online store, app store and business pages, and to WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. When you follow one of these links, you leave this website and the other service's own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external services. Our free Android apps may show advertisements served by Google AdMob; ad content is controlled by Google and its advertisers, not by us.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before ordering from our store or contacting us.

Your choices

You can contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about your order or message, or to ask us to delete it. You can also opt out of personalised ads and analytics using the links above, and you can disable cookies in your browser.
