About Us

Jilan Apps Official is a small digital studio based in Basti Malook, District Multan, Punjab, Pakistan. We build simple, useful mobile apps and online stores for everyday people and local businesses. The studio is run by Dr Asif, who designs and builds the apps himself. It is a small, independent setup — not a big company — and when you contact us, you talk directly to the person doing the work.

Why we started

Most apps and websites are made for big cities and big budgets. We started Jilan Apps Official because people in small towns and villages also need good technology — apps that are easy to use, light on the phone, and available in a language people understand. Our apps are free, our store takes Cash on Delivery, and our business work is priced for small shops and clinics, not for large companies. We explain things step by step, in English or Urdu, and we are happy to help people who are using a smartphone for the first time.

What we do

Mobile apps: our Jilan App Store offers more than 200 free Android apps — tools, utilities and everyday helpers made by us. They include Rah-e-Noor, our Islamic app with the Holy Quran, duas, prayer times and a tasbeeh counter; Jilan All Video Downloader, for saving videos to watch offline; Status Studio, for creating photo statuses and poetry posters; Jilan Time Lock, a screen-time helper; and Jilan Prompt Maker, a tool for writing better prompts for AI apps. We keep our apps simple and light so they run well even on older phones.

Online shopping: our Online Shopping Store sells a wide range of everyday products at fair prices. Orders are placed on WhatsApp — every product has an item code, and you simply send the code with your name and delivery address. Payment is Cash on Delivery, so you pay when your order arrives. There is no account to create and no card payment needed. See our guide, How to order from our store on WhatsApp, for the full steps.

Business websites and apps: we build mobile apps and websites for local shops, clinics, schools, restaurants and small businesses — see our Business page for details. If you run a business and want an app or a website, tell us what you need on WhatsApp or by email. We discuss the details with you, build a first version, and adjust it with your feedback until it is right. Our guide How to request an app or website for your business explains what to tell us.

How we work

We keep things simple and honest. Our apps do the job they promise and nothing extra. We do not sell your information, and we do not ask for permissions or details we do not need. If something we make does not work properly, tell us and we will try to fix it. If you have an idea for a new app, tell us too — many of our apps started as a request from a user.
