Our App Store — Free Android Apps

The Jilan App Store is our collection of free Android apps — more than 200 apps made by us, all free to download. We build tools and utilities for everyday use, and every app is made to do one job well, without unnecessary complications.

Some of our most-used apps are Rah-e-Noor, our Islamic app with the Holy Quran, duas, prayer times and a tasbeeh counter; Jilan All Video Downloader, which lets you save videos from social media to watch offline; Status Studio, for creating photo statuses and poetry posters; Jilan Time Lock, a simple screen-time and app-lock helper; and Jilan Prompt Maker, a tool for writing better prompts for AI apps. You can browse the full store, read what each app does, and download the ones you need. When a new version of an app is available, you can download it from the same store page and install it over the old version — there is no need to uninstall first.

We keep our apps simple and light, so they run well even on older phones. If an app does not work as expected, or you have an idea for a new app, message us on WhatsApp and tell us — many of our apps started as a request from a user.

Online Shopping Store

Our Online Shopping Store sells everyday products at fair prices. You can browse the store on your phone, choose what you need, and order it on WhatsApp — there is no account to create and no card payment needed. Every product has an item code, for example JLN-123456. To order, just send us the item code on WhatsApp, along with your name and delivery address. We confirm the price and delivery details with you, and you pay in cash when your order arrives — Cash on Delivery.

If you have a question about a product — its size, colour, stock, or delivery time — message us on WhatsApp before ordering and we will confirm it for you. If there is ever a problem with your order, contact us the same way and we will help sort it out.

Apps and Websites for Local Businesses

We build mobile apps and websites for local businesses in and around Multan. If you run a shop, clinic, school, restaurant, or any small business, we can build something that helps your customers find you and understand what you offer. That could be a simple app for your shop, a website that shows your products or services, or a page where customers can contact you directly.

The process is simple. Tell us about your business on WhatsApp or by email, and explain what you need the app or website to do. There are no hidden steps — you see the work as it is being made, and you only use it when you are happy with it.
