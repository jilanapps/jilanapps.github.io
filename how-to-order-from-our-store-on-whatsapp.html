How to Order from Our Store on WhatsApp

Ordering from the Jilan Apps Official Online Shopping Store is simple. There is no account to create and no card payment needed — you order on WhatsApp and pay in cash when your order arrives (Cash on Delivery). Here are the steps.

Step 1: Find the product

Open the Online Shopping Store on your phone and browse the products. When you find something you like, tap on it to see the full details — the price, the available colours or sizes, and the delivery information.

Step 2: Note the item code

Every product in our store has an item code, for example JLN-123456. Write the code down, or take a screenshot of the product. The item code tells us exactly which product you want, so there is no confusion about size, colour or model.

Step 3: Send us a WhatsApp message

Message us on WhatsApp at 0300 7379385. In your message, include:

  • The item code of the product you want
  • How many pieces you want
  • Your full name
  • Your complete delivery address

For example: "Assalam-o-Alaikum, I want to order JLN-123456, 1 piece. My name is Ahmed, my address is Chak 12-F, Basti Malook, Multan."

Step 4: We confirm your order

We reply with the total price, including delivery charges, and confirm that the product is in stock. If the product is out of stock, or if you asked about a size or colour we do not have, we will tell you honestly before taking your order. If everything is fine, we prepare your parcel for dispatch.

Step 5: Pay Cash on Delivery

When your order arrives, check the parcel, then pay in cash to the delivery person. That is all — you never need to pay anything in advance, and we never ask for your card or bank details.

Tips for a smooth order

  • Always send the item code — it is faster and more accurate than describing the product.
  • If you are unsure about size, colour or stock, ask us on WhatsApp before ordering.
  • Give your complete address, including your town or village name, so the delivery reaches you without delay.
  • If there is a problem with your order when it arrives, message us on WhatsApp right away and we will help sort it out.
