What to tell us

To help us answer you quickly, please include the right details in your first message:

  • To order a product: the product's item code (for example JLN-123456), your name, and your delivery address. See our guide, How to order from our store on WhatsApp.
  • To report a problem with an app: the name of the app, what happened, and the model of your phone if you know it. A screenshot helps if you can send one.
  • To ask for an app or website for your business: the name and type of your business (shop, clinic, school, restaurant, or other), and what you want the app or website to do. See our guide, How to request an app or website for your business.
  • For anything else: just tell us what you need, in your own words, and we will do our best to help.

When will we reply?

We try to reply to every message as soon as we can, usually the same day. If your message is about an order, please send it during the day so we can confirm the details and dispatch it without delay.
