How to Request an App or Website for Your Business

If you run a shop, clinic, school, restaurant, or any small business, we can build you a mobile app or a website. This guide explains what to tell us, and how the work happens — so you know exactly what to expect before you message us.

Who is this for?

Our business work is for local businesses in and around Multan — for example, a shop that wants customers to see its products, a clinic that wants patients to find its timings and services, a school that wants to share information with parents, or a restaurant that wants people to see its menu and contact details. If you are not sure whether an app or a website is right for you, message us anyway and we will talk it through with you.

Step 1: Tell us about your business

Message us on WhatsApp at 0300 7379385 or email jilanappsofficial@gmail.com. In your first message, tell us:

  • The name of your business and what it does
  • Whether you want a mobile app, a website, or both
  • What you want it to show — for example, your products, your services, your timings, your location, or a way for customers to contact you
  • Any example you like — for instance, another shop's app or website you have seen and want something similar to

You can write in English or Urdu. Do not worry about technical words — just describe what you need in your own words.

Step 2: We discuss the details with you

We reply with our questions, so we understand exactly what you need. We also tell you what we can build, how it will work, and what it will cost. The cost and the details of the work are agreed with you before we start building — there are no hidden charges added later.

Step 3: We build a first version

We build a first version of your app or website and share it with you to try. You can open it on your own phone, show it to your family or staff, and see how it looks and works.

Step 4: You tell us what to change

After trying the first version, tell us what you like and what you want changed — colours, wording, photos, extra pages, or anything else. We adjust it until it is right for you. This back-and-forth is a normal part of the work, so do not hesitate to ask for changes.

Step 5: Your app or website is ready

When you are happy with it, your app or website is finished and ready to use. The app or website we build for you belongs to your business, and you may use it for your business. If you need a change later — a new product, a new price, or a new page — message us and we will help.

What we have built before

We have built apps and websites for shops, clinics and community organisations — including a blood donor app for a local welfare society, with donor records, certificates and a verification system. Every project is different, but the way we work is the same: simple, honest, and built around what your business actually needs.
