How to Install an APK from Our App Store

Our apps are free Android apps, and you download them from the Jilan App Store as APK files. An APK is simply the file Android uses to install an app. Because our apps come from our own store and not from the Google Play Store, your phone will ask for your permission once before installing. This guide explains each step.

Step 1: Download the app from our store

Open the Jilan App Store on your phone, find the app you want, and tap the download button. The APK file will download to your phone. Wait until the download finishes completely before doing anything else.

Step 2: Allow installation from your browser

When you tap the downloaded file, Android may show a message saying installation is blocked, because the file did not come from the Play Store. This is normal. Tap Settings on that message, then turn on "Allow from this source" for your browser (usually Chrome). This permission only allows your browser to install apps — it does not change anything else on your phone. You only need to do this once; after that, your phone will remember it.

Step 3: Install the app

Go back and tap the downloaded file again, then tap Install. Wait for the installation to finish, then tap Open to use the app, or find its icon on your home screen or in your app drawer.

How to update an app

When a new version of an app is available, download it from the same store page and install it the same way. You do not need to uninstall the old version first — the new version installs over it, and your settings and saved data stay as they are.

  • Check the app name and icon before installing, so you know it is the app you wanted.

  • Read the permission request. When you open an app for the first time, it may ask for a permission it needs to work — for example, storage access to save a file. Only allow what makes sense for what the app does.

If installation does not work

If the app does not install, check these three things: first, make sure the download finished fully — a half-downloaded file will not install. Second, check that you have enough free storage on your phone. Third, make sure you allowed installation from your browser in Step 2.
